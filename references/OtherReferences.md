# 2. WHO — excellent for understanding the "big picture"

[WHO — Universal Health Coverage](https://www.who.int/health-topics/universal-health-coverage?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The WHO defines healthcare much more broadly than "going to the doctor."

It includes:

**prevention → diagnosis → treatment → rehabilitation → palliative care**

and emphasizes that healthcare systems have to solve two problems simultaneously:

**Can people get the care they need?**

and

**Can they afford it without financial hardship?**

That's almost exactly the conceptual foundation of your site. ([World Health Organization][3])

I'd use WHO as your **conceptual authority**, rather than as the main source for country-by-country details.

# What I think is missing from existing sites

This is where I think your idea has real potential.

Most existing resources fall into one of three categories:

### A. Medical websites

> "What is breast cancer?"

Very good at explaining disease.

### B. Healthcare-policy websites

> "Healthcare expenditure as a percentage of GDP..."

Very good at explaining systems.

### C. Insurance websites

> "What is a deductible?"

Very good at explaining financial terminology.

But there is much less content answering:

> **"I am a 52-year-old woman and I just discovered a lump. What happens to me from today until I'm healthy again?"**

And then:

> **"What would happen if I lived in India?"**

> **"What would happen if I lived in the US?"**

> **"Who would I see?"**

> **"How long would it take?"**

> **"Who would pay?"**

> **"What happens if I don't have insurance?"**

> **"What happens if I live in a village rather than a major city?"**

> **"What happens to my family?"**

That **patient journey** could be the distinctive feature of your site.

## A model I'd seriously consider

I'd make every major topic have **three layers**:

### Layer 1 — Explain it

**"What is health insurance?"**

Simple 2–3 minute explanation.

### Layer 2 — Show it

**"Here's what happens when you get breast cancer."**

Interactive patient journey.

### Layer 3 — Compare it

**USA 🇺🇸 vs India 🇮🇳 vs UK 🇬🇧 vs Germany 🇩🇪**

Same patient. Same disease. Different healthcare system.

And underneath each comparison:

**Medical | Financial | Access | Time | Family | Technology | Outcome**

That would make your site substantially different from most of the resources above.

If you're going to build this seriously, I'd recommend we next **reverse-engineer 5–7 of these sites**—looking at their navigation, content structure, visualizations, strengths and weaknesses—and then design the information architecture for your site so that we're *learning from them without simply reproducing them*.
