# Teeth — quick-reference card

**What they do:** Bite, chew, and help you speak clearly; they also shape your face. Enamel — the outer layer — is the hardest substance in the human body.

**What can go wrong:** Tooth decay (cavities) · gum disease (gingivitis → periodontitis) · tooth abscess · cracked or broken tooth · impacted wisdom teeth · tooth loss · mouth sores that don't heal (can signal oral cancer).

**Doctors:** General dentist · orthodontist (alignment) · endodontist (root canals) · periodontist (gums) · oral & maxillofacial surgeon · dental hygienist (cleaning & prevention).

**Usual tests:** Dental exam · X-rays (bitewing, panoramic) · pulp vitality test · gum-pocket probing.

**Keep them healthy:**
- Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- Clean between teeth once a day (floss or interdental brushes)
- Limit sugary and acidic drinks
- Dental check-up regularly, even with no pain

**See a dentist soon:** Severe or lingering toothache · bleeding gums · loose adult tooth · mouth sore not healed in 2 weeks.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Facial swelling with fever, or trouble swallowing/breathing · uncontrolled bleeding · a knocked-out adult tooth (minutes matter — get to a dentist within the hour).
