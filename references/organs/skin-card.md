### Quick-reference card: Skin

**What it does:** The body's largest organ — about 2 square metres in an adult. It protects against germs and injury, controls body temperature, keeps water in, and lets you feel touch, heat, and pain.

**What can go wrong:** Acne · eczema (atopic dermatitis) · psoriasis · fungal infections (ringworm, athlete's foot) · hives · skin cancer, including melanoma.

**Doctors:** Dermatologist (skin specialist) · primary care doctor · allergist (hives, some eczema) · surgeon (removing skin cancers).

**Usual tests:** Skin exam, sometimes with a dermatoscope (magnifier) · skin biopsy · patch testing (allergies) · skin scraping or culture (fungal infections).

**Keep it healthy:**
- Protect from the sun: shade, clothing, sunscreen
- Moisturise dry skin; cleanse gently
- Don't pick, squeeze, or scratch spots and moles
- Check your moles periodically (see ABCDE below)

**See a doctor soon:** A mole that changes (ABCDE: Asymmetry, uneven Border, Colour changes, Diameter growing, Evolving) · a rash that doesn't improve · persistent itching · a sore that won't heal.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Rapidly spreading skin redness with fever (possible cellulitis) · signs of severe allergic reaction: swelling of lips or tongue, trouble breathing, widespread hives with dizziness.
