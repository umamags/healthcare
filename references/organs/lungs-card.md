### Quick-reference card: Lungs

**What they do:** Pull oxygen from the air into the blood and push carbon dioxide out — tens of thousands of breaths a day. Deep inside, tiny air sacs (alveoli) do the actual gas exchange across a huge surface area.

**What can go wrong:** Asthma · COPD (chronic bronchitis and emphysema) · pneumonia · tuberculosis (TB) · bronchitis · lung cancer.

**Doctors:** Pulmonologist (lung specialist) · thoracic surgeon · infectious disease specialist (TB, pneumonia) · allergist (asthma) · primary care doctor.

**Usual tests:** Chest X-ray · CT scan · spirometry (blowing test for lung function) · pulse oximetry (oxygen level on a fingertip clip) · sputum test · bronchoscopy (camera into the airways).

**Keep them healthy:**
- Don't smoke or vape; avoid secondhand smoke
- Limit exposure to polluted or dusty air where you can
- Exercise regularly — it trains your breathing muscles
- Get recommended vaccines (flu, pneumonia) where available
- If treated for TB: finish the entire course of medicines

**See a doctor soon:** A cough lasting more than 3 weeks · wheezing or whistling breath · coughing up small amounts of blood · cough with unexplained weight loss or night sweats (possible TB).
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Severe breathlessness, or can't finish a sentence · coughing up a lot of blood · bluish lips or face · chest pain with breathing difficulty.
