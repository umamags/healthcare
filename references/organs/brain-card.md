# Brain — quick-reference card

**What it does:** The body's control center — runs thought, memory, movement, the senses, and automatic functions like breathing and heartbeat. Tens of billions of nerve cells (neurons) pass messages electrically and chemically.

**What can go wrong:** Stroke · dementia (e.g. Alzheimer's disease) · Parkinson's disease · epilepsy · migraine · brain tumours · traumatic brain injury (concussion and worse). Note: mental-health conditions such as depression and anxiety are usually managed with psychiatrists, a separate specialty.

**Doctors:** Neurologist (brain & nervous system disorders) · neurosurgeon (brain operations) · psychiatrist (mental-health conditions, often working alongside neurology).

**Usual tests:** Neurological exam · CT scan · MRI · EEG (brain-wave recording) · lumbar puncture (spinal tap).

**Keep it healthy:**
- Regular physical activity; keep blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar in check
- Don't smoke; limit alcohol
- Protect your head — helmets, seatbelts
- Stay mentally and socially active; get hearing loss treated

**See a doctor soon:** Headaches that are new, frequent, or changing pattern · memory problems affecting daily life · tremor, stiffness, or slowed movement · a first seizure (even if you feel fine afterwards).
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now (FAST):** Face drooping · Arm weakness · Speech difficulty · Time to call. Also: sudden "worst headache of my life" · seizure lasting more than ~5 minutes · head injury with confusion or vomiting.
