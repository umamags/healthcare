# Spinal cord — quick-reference card

**What it does:** The information highway between brain and body — a bundle of nerves running down inside the spine, carrying movement commands down and sensation up. It also handles quick reflexes on its own.

**What can go wrong:** Slipped (herniated) disc · spinal stenosis (narrowing of the spinal canal) · sciatica (nerve pain down the leg) · spinal cord injury from trauma · muscle/ligament strain (the cause of most back pain — not the cord itself).

**Doctors:** Neurologist (nerve function) · neurosurgeon or orthopedic spine surgeon (spine operations) · physiotherapist (rehabilitation) · pain specialist.

**Usual tests:** Neurological exam (strength, reflexes, sensation) · MRI · CT or X-ray (bones) · sometimes nerve conduction studies.

**Keep it healthy:**
- Stay active and strengthen your core muscles
- Lift with your legs, not your back
- Keep a healthy weight
- Don't smoke — spinal discs get poorer blood supply in smokers

**See a doctor soon:** Back or neck pain with numbness or weakness in an arm or leg · pain not improving after a few weeks · pain after a fall or accident.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** New loss of bladder or bowel control · numbness around the genitals or inner thighs ("saddle" area) · rapidly worsening leg weakness. Together these can signal cauda equina syndrome — a surgical emergency where hours matter.
