# Nose — quick-reference card

**What it does:** Warms, moistens, and filters the air you breathe; houses the sense of smell; drains the sinuses (air-filled spaces in the skull bones).

**What can go wrong:** Allergic rhinitis (hay fever) · sinusitis (acute or chronic) · deviated septum · nosebleeds · nasal polyps · loss of smell.

**Doctors:** ENT specialist (otolaryngologist) · allergist/immunologist (allergy-driven problems).

**Usual tests:** Nasal exam (sometimes with a thin endoscope) · allergy testing (skin or blood) · CT scan of the sinuses (for chronic or surgical cases).

**Keep it healthy:**
- Manage allergies rather than just enduring them
- Saline rinses can ease congestion
- Don't smoke; humidify very dry indoor air
- Blow your nose gently — hard blowing can worsen sinus pressure

**See a doctor soon:** Congestion lasting more than ~10 days or repeatedly returning · frequent nosebleeds · persistent loss of smell · one-sided blockage that never clears.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now / seek emergency care:** A nosebleed not stopping after ~20 minutes of proper pressure · difficulty breathing through swelling or injury · heavy bleeding after a head injury.
