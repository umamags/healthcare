### Quick-reference card: Bones (skeletal system)

**What they do:** Your 206 bones form the skeleton — the frame that holds you up, protects organs (skull, ribs, spine), anchors muscles, and makes blood cells inside the marrow. Bone is living tissue that constantly breaks down and rebuilds itself.

**What can go wrong:** Fractures (broken bones) · osteoporosis (bones thinning with age) · osteoarthritis (wear-and-tear joint damage) · rheumatoid arthritis (immune system attacking joints) · bone infection · bone cancer (rare).

**Doctors:** Orthopedic doctor/surgeon (bones, joints, injuries) · rheumatologist (arthritis, especially autoimmune) · primary care doctor (screening, referrals).

**Usual tests:** X-ray · DEXA scan (bone density) · MRI · CT scan · blood tests (calcium, vitamin D, inflammation markers).

**Keep them healthy:**
- Weight-bearing exercise: walking, dancing, light resistance training
- Enough calcium and vitamin D (food plus safe sun exposure)
- Don't smoke; limit alcohol
- Prevent falls: good lighting, sturdy footwear, clear walkways

**See a doctor soon:** Joint pain, swelling, or stiffness lasting weeks · loss of height or a stooped posture · back pain that doesn't ease.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** A limb that looks deformed after an injury · bone visible through broken skin (open fracture) · inability to move or bear weight after trauma · a limb that turns pale, cold, or numb below an injury.
