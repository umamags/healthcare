# Epididymis & vas deferens (sperm ducts) — quick-reference card

**What they do:** The epididymis is a tightly coiled tube sitting on the back of each testicle — sperm mature and are stored there. The vas deferens is the firm cord-like tube that carries sperm from the epididymis toward the urethra during ejaculation.

**What can go wrong:** Epididymitis (painful inflammation, often infection) · epididymal cysts / spermatocele · blocked vas deferens (a cause of infertility) · post-vasectomy pain syndrome.

**Doctors:** Urologist · andrologist / fertility specialist.

**Usual tests:** Physical exam · urine and STI tests · scrotal ultrasound · semen analysis.

**Keep them healthy:**
- Protect against STIs and get tested/treat infections early
- Treat urinary infections promptly
- Seek care quickly for scrotal pain or swelling

**See a doctor soon:** Aching scrotal pain or swelling that builds over days · a new lump on the back of the testicle · pain after passing urine · difficulty conceiving.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Sudden severe scrotal pain (a twisted testicle must be ruled out first — see the testes page) · scrotal pain with high fever.
