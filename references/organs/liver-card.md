### Quick-reference card: Liver

**What it does:** The body's chemical factory — around 500 jobs. It filters blood, makes bile to digest fats, stores energy, processes medicines and alcohol, and makes proteins that help blood clot. It can regrow damaged tissue, up to a point.

**What can go wrong:** Fatty liver disease (now called MASLD — renamed from NAFLD in 2023, so you may see both names) · hepatitis A, B, and C · alcohol-related liver disease · cirrhosis (scarring) · liver cancer.

**Doctors:** Hepatologist (liver specialist) · gastroenterologist · infectious disease specialist (hepatitis) · transplant surgeon (advanced disease).

**Usual tests:** Liver function blood tests · hepatitis blood tests · abdominal ultrasound · elastography/FibroScan (measures liver stiffness) · CT or MRI · liver biopsy.

**Keep it healthy:**
- Limit alcohol; avoid it entirely if you have liver disease
- Keep a healthy weight — fatty liver is closely tied to it
- Get hepatitis A and B vaccines where available
- Avoid sharing needles, razors, or toothbrushes (hepatitis B/C spread through blood)
- Be cautious with herbal supplements — some harm the liver

**See a doctor soon:** Yellowing of skin or eyes (jaundice) · dark urine with pale stools · persistent fatigue · swelling of the belly or ankles · itchy skin with no rash.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Vomiting blood · black, tarry stools · sudden confusion or extreme drowsiness in someone with liver disease · severe abdominal pain with fever.
