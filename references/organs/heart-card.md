# Heart — quick-reference card

**What it does:** A fist-sized muscular pump that beats about 100,000 times a day, pushing blood — carrying oxygen and nutrients — to every part of your body. Four chambers, four valves, its own electrical system, and coronary arteries that feed the heart muscle itself.

**What can go wrong:** Coronary artery disease / angina · heart attack · heart failure · arrhythmias (e.g. atrial fibrillation) · valve disease · congenital (from-birth) defects. Note: high blood pressure is a major risk factor, not a heart disease itself.

**Doctors:** Cardiologist · interventional cardiologist (stents) · electrophysiologist (rhythm) · cardiac surgeon · your primary care doctor, who usually coordinates.

**Usual tests:** Blood pressure · ECG (electrocardiogram) · echocardiogram (heart ultrasound) · stress test · blood tests (troponin, cholesterol/lipid panel) · Holter monitor (24–48 hr rhythm recording) · coronary angiogram.

**Keep it healthy:**
- 150 minutes of moderate activity per week
- Vegetables, whole grains; less salt and saturated fat
- No tobacco; limit alcohol
- Keep blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar in check
- 7–9 hours of sleep; know your family history

**See a doctor soon:** Palpitations or fluttering · swollen ankles · unusual fatigue · waking up short of breath.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Chest pain, pressure, or tightness · pain spreading to arm, neck, jaw, or back · sudden shortness of breath · cold sweat or nausea with chest discomfort · fainting. Don't drive yourself.
