### Quick-reference card: Stomach

**What it does:** A muscular sac that churns food and mixes it with strong acid and enzymes, turning it into a thick liquid for the intestines. The acid also kills many germs in food.

**What can go wrong:** Acid reflux / GERD · gastritis (lining inflammation) · stomach ulcers (often caused by H. pylori bacteria or painkiller medicines) · indigestion (dyspepsia) · stomach cancer.

**Doctors:** Gastroenterologist (digestive specialist) · general surgeon · primary care doctor.

**Usual tests:** Endoscopy (a thin camera passed down the throat to look inside) · H. pylori breath, stool, or blood test · biopsy during endoscopy · abdominal ultrasound · CT scan.

**Keep it healthy:**
- Eat regular, unhurried meals; stop before you're stuffed
- Ease off foods and drinks that trigger your symptoms
- Don't smoke; limit alcohol
- Use NSAID painkillers only as directed — ask your doctor about stomach protection if you need them regularly

**See a doctor soon:** Frequent heartburn or acid taste · gnawing upper-belly pain · nausea or feeling full very quickly · unexplained weight loss or loss of appetite.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Vomiting blood or material that looks like coffee grounds · black, tarry stools · sudden, severe abdominal pain.
