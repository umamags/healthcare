### Quick-reference card: Mouth

**What it does:** The entry point of digestion — lips, cheeks, and tongue move food around while saliva starts breaking it down and keeps the mouth moist. The tongue also handles taste and much of speech.

**What can go wrong:** Mouth ulcers (canker sores) · cold sores · oral thrush (yeast infection) · dry mouth · persistent bad breath · oral cancer.

**Doctors:** Dentist · oral & maxillofacial surgeon · ENT specialist (otolaryngologist) · primary care doctor.

**Usual tests:** Oral exam · swab or culture (thrush) · biopsy of sores that don't heal · blood tests · imaging (X-ray/CT) if cancer is suspected.

**Keep it healthy:**
- Brush teeth and gently clean the tongue daily
- Drink enough water; saliva protects your mouth
- Avoid tobacco; limit alcohol
- See a dentist regularly, even with no pain

**See a doctor or dentist soon:** An ulcer or sore not healed in 2 weeks · white or red patches inside the mouth · dry mouth that makes eating or speaking hard · bad breath that won't go away.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Rapidly growing swelling in the mouth, face, or neck · trouble swallowing or breathing · bleeding that won't stop.
