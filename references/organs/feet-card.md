### Quick-reference card: Feet

**What they do:** Like hands, feet aren't a single organ — each foot packs 26 bones, springy arches, tendons (including the Achilles, the body's strongest), and nerves into a platform that carries your full weight, balances you, and absorbs shock with every step.

**What can go wrong:** Plantar fasciitis (heel pain) · bunions · athlete's foot (a fungal skin infection) · ingrown toenails · ankle sprains · diabetes-related foot problems (nerve damage plus slow healing).

**Doctors:** Podiatrist (foot specialist) · orthopedic foot & ankle surgeon · primary care doctor · your diabetes care team, if relevant.

**Usual tests:** Physical exam and gait check · X-ray · ultrasound · MRI · foot sensation test (monofilament, used in diabetes).

**Keep them healthy:**
- Wear supportive, well-fitting shoes; replace them when worn out
- Keep feet clean and dry; change socks after sweating
- Trim toenails straight across, not curved
- If you have diabetes: check your feet daily and never walk barefoot

**See a doctor soon:** Heel pain lasting weeks · a bunion that's getting worse · a blister, cut, or sore on the foot that won't heal (especially with diabetes) · recurring ingrown toenails.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Sudden inability to bear weight after a twist or fall · any foot injury in a person with diabetes with spreading redness, swelling, or warmth · a foot that turns pale, cold, or numb after injury.
