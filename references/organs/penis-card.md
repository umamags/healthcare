# Penis — quick-reference card

**What it does:** The penis has two jobs: passing urine out of the body, and sexual function — becoming firm (an erection) for intercourse and releasing semen (ejaculation). Its main parts are the shaft, the glans (head), the foreskin (in uncircumcised men), and the opening of the urethra at the tip.

**What can go wrong:** Erectile dysfunction (trouble getting or keeping an erection) · Peyronie's disease (a curved or bent erection) · balanitis (inflammation of the glans/foreskin) · phimosis (foreskin too tight to pull back) · paraphimosis (foreskin stuck behind the glans) · sexually transmitted infections · penile cancer (rare).

**Doctors:** Primary care doctor (usually first) · urologist · andrologist (male sexual/reproductive health) · dermatologist (skin conditions) · sexual health doctor.

**Usual tests:** Physical exam · blood tests (blood sugar, testosterone, cholesterol) · penile ultrasound · STI tests.

**Keep it healthy:**
- Don't smoke — erections depend on healthy blood flow
- Manage blood pressure, diabetes, cholesterol, and weight
- Practise safer sex
- Wash gently, including under the foreskin — and always pull the foreskin back into place afterwards

**See a doctor soon:** Erection problems that keep happening · a new curve, bend, or lump in the shaft · a red, sore, or itchy glans · a foreskin that won't pull back · any sore, lump, or patch that doesn't heal.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** An erection lasting more than about 4 hours · a foreskin stuck behind the glans with swelling · a sudden snapping pain with rapid swelling and bruising during sex · severe injury or bleeding that won't stop.
