# Ears — quick-reference card

**What they do:** Two jobs — hearing and balance. The outer and middle ear collect sound and pass vibrations inward; the inner ear's cochlea turns them into nerve signals. The inner ear's vestibular system keeps you balanced.

**What can go wrong:** Hearing loss (age-related or noise-induced) · middle-ear infection / otitis media (common in children) · swimmer's ear (outer-ear infection) · tinnitus (ringing or buzzing) · vertigo / Ménière's disease · earwax blockage · perforated eardrum.

**Doctors:** ENT specialist (otolaryngologist) · audiologist (hearing tests and hearing aids) · primary care doctor (simple infections).

**Usual tests:** Otoscopy (looking inside the ear) · hearing test (audiometry) · tympanometry (eardrum movement) · balance assessment.

**Keep them healthy:**
- Protect your ears from loud noise — earplugs at concerts, machinery, firearms
- Never push cotton buds deep into the ear canal
- Dry your ears after swimming
- Get ear infections treated promptly; get your hearing checked if you notice loss

**See a doctor soon:** Gradual hearing loss · persistent ringing · repeated ear infections · dizziness that affects daily life · ear pain with fever.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now / seek emergency care:** Sudden hearing loss in one or both ears (a same-day emergency — treatment works best within days) · severe ear pain or bleeding after an injury · dizziness with weakness or speech difficulty (think stroke — call emergency).
