# Cervix — quick-reference card

**What it does:** The neck of the womb — a short, muscular passage between the uterus and the top of the vagina. It makes mucus that changes across the cycle, helps keep bacteria out during pregnancy, and stretches open (dilates) during labour.

**What can go wrong:** Cervicitis (inflammation, often from infection) · cervical polyps (small, usually benign growths) · HPV infection (very common, usually clears on its own) · precancerous cell changes (CIN) · cervical cancer.

**Doctors:** Gynaecologist · colposcopist (examines the cervix under magnification) · gynaecologic oncologist (cancer) · screening is usually arranged through primary care.

**Usual tests:** Pap test (smear) · HPV test · colposcopy · cervical biopsy.

**Keep it healthy:**
- Get the HPV vaccine — follow your local programme
- Attend regular cervical screening, as your local programme recommends
- Safer sex lowers the risk of HPV and other infections
- Don't smoke — smoking raises cervical cancer risk

**See a doctor soon:** Bleeding after sex or between periods · unusual vaginal discharge · ongoing pelvic pain.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** Heavy bleeding · any bleeding during pregnancy · severe pelvic pain with fever.
