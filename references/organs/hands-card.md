### Quick-reference card: Hands

**What they do:** Hands aren't a single organ — each hand is a team of 27 bones, joints, tendons, muscles, and nerves working together for grip, precision, and touch. Your two hands hold about a quarter of all the bones in your body.

**What can go wrong:** Carpal tunnel syndrome (a wrist nerve under pressure) · arthritis, especially at the thumb base · trigger finger (a finger that catches or locks) · tendon injuries · fractures and sprains.

**Doctors:** Orthopedic hand surgeon · rheumatologist (inflammatory arthritis) · hand therapist / physiotherapist (rehab, splints) · primary care doctor.

**Usual tests:** Physical exam, grip and strength tests · X-ray · ultrasound (tendons) · MRI · nerve conduction study (for carpal tunnel).

**Keep them healthy:**
- Take breaks from repetitive gripping or typing; vary your tasks
- Keep wrists straight, not bent, during long tasks
- Strengthen your grip gently and stretch your fingers
- Protect your hands: gloves for work, guards for sport

**See a doctor soon:** A finger that catches or locks when bending · persistent numbness or tingling in the fingers · joint pain and stiffness lasting weeks · night-time hand numbness that wakes you.
**Emergency — call your local emergency number now:** A deep cut with numbness, or a finger you suddenly can't move (possible tendon or nerve damage) · a deformed finger after a jam or fall · bleeding that won't stop with firm pressure.
